Setup

This app requires each group member to install a runelite plugin from the Plugin Hub in order to track player information. Find it by searching "GroupScape" in the Runelite client.

The plugin links to your GroupScape account, not directly to a group. Create an account if you don't have one, then paste your account's API key (found on your account page) into the plugin's Account Config section.

Once you're in-game, your character will show up as pending on your characters page — confirm it there. Then come back here and use the Group Token above to assign that confirmed character to this group from your characters page.
